One pen stroke.

The Four Notes brand is small on purpose: a mark drawn in one movement, an ink frame, paper sheets, and three colours that always mean something.

Download the logos

The mark

Traced from one movement of a pen. The stroke is navy on paper and white on ink. The spark is always red.

  • Keep it clear.Leave one spark's width of space on every side.
  • Keep it legible.Never smaller than 16 pixels.
  • Keep it whole.Do not redraw, tilt, outline or recolour the stroke.

Six marks

One row for everything. The mark on the left says what it is and what state it is in, so the row needs no badges.

Colour means something

The frame recedes so the sheets carry the content. Red means today, now or late. Amber means important. Violet means the assistant suggested it. Everything else stays neutral.
